Biography

I'm an M.Sc. student in Electrical Engineering (Control) at K.N. Toosi University of Technology, working toward my thesis on explainable transfer learning for fault diagnosis of rotating machinery using RGB-encoded vibration signals, under the supervision of Dr. Mahdi Aliyari-Shoorehdeli. As a research assistant at the Fault Detection and Identification (FDI) laboratory, I work with real-world industrial datasets and co-author journal papers on machine and deep learning applied to structural health monitoring, fault diagnosis, and time-series analysis. My broader interests span federated learning, explainable AI, transfer learning, large language models, and vision-language models, and I also serve as a teaching assistant for Machine Learning and Principles of Artificial Intelligence.
